I'm a complete beginner. Is that OK?

Yes. Lessons start from where you are. Beginners speak from the first lesson using simple, useful phrases.

Are lessons online or in person?

Online.

Which levels do you teach?

English and Polish from A1 to C2, Chinese from HSK 1 to HSK 6.

1:1 or small group: which is right for me?

1:1 is best for fast progress and specific goals. Small groups are great for speaking practice with others. Not sure? Ask us.

Do you teach teenagers?

Yes, English for teenagers.

Can my company pay?

Yes. We issue invoices for companies, and business English and Chinese can be tailored to your team.
